## Register

`brand` — corporate marketing/communications site for RWE AG, a leading
global renewable-energy company.

## Product Purpose

Corporate presence: communicates RWE's energy-transition strategy
("Our energy for a sustainable life", climate-neutral by 2040), investor
information (share price, financial publications, press releases), careers
("International, open, diverse – working at RWE"), and entry points into
products/services, projects, and country sites.

## Users

Investors and analysts, journalists, job seekers, business partners,
neighbours/communities near RWE sites, and the interested public.

## Brand Personality

Confident, forward-looking, responsible. Corporate but warm — photography of
real workers and landscapes; teal/navy energy palette; slogan "Our energy for
a sustainable life."
