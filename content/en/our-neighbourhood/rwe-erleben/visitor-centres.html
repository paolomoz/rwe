Our Visitor Centres

We welcome visitors, neighbors and tourists to join us

The reliable availability of electricity is becoming increasingly important for the interaction of all electrified areas in our society. A mix of electricity from conventional fossil and renewable sources such as wind and sun is indispensable. Over the past 20 years, we have continuously modernised our power plant fleet and increased the efficiency of our plants. With the newly added wind farms and solar plants, we are supplementing our portfolio for a sustainable energy future. We would like to invite visitors, neighbors and tourists to visit our international information centers and convince themselves of the power of renewable energy.
