Floating Offshore Wind in Spain

DemoSATH

style
article-stage

In Spain, we are demonstrating the SATH concept, which is a concrete twin-hull barge structure, made of modular, pre-fabricated components. The design has a single point of mooring, which means it aligns itself to the current and wave direction.

The DemoSATH project uses a 2MW turbine which was assembled in the port of Bilbao in northern Spain. The platform, including the turbine, has been towed to the BiMEP test site, which is 2 miles offshore with a water depth of 85m. Hybrid mooring lines, composed of chains and fibre, anchored to the seabed hold the unit in position.

Status: In operation since September 2023

DemoSATH Lab

We’ve collaborated with Saitec to commission enhanced environmental monitoring studies at our DemoSATH demonstrator. 

The DemoSATH Lab initiative will study the platform's interactions with the environment focusing on the possible impact on birds, the emission of underwater noise and the interactions of marine life with the structure. The projects will help us to increase our understanding of key biodiversity aspects of floating offshore wind turbines and ensure our future development of floating offshore wind is undertaken in coexistence with nature.

The programme will last until at least the end of 2025 in order to span multiple seasons and weather conditions.
