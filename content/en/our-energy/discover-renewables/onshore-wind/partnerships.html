The road to success

RWE Renewables Europe & Australia is a world leader in the construction and operation of onshore wind farms. As a current and future energy utility, we not only plan, develop and construct these facilities, but also operate them ourselves. An important element in our success in this regard is our collaboration with local project partners.

In the area of wind power, new project development is growing more complex, and regulatory demands have also increased. The associated procedures are thus becoming much more protracted, uncertain and labour-intensive.

That makes it all the more important to have a powerful partner at your side who will work with you as an equal at all times.

RWE – a reliable and professional project partner

With us you get a partner that’s there to support you with all your concerns at all times. With our professional knowledge, many years of experience, broad-based skills and many contacts within the industry, we will make a success of your project idea. We start where you need individual support, from the early stages of project development through to implementation and the grid connection. Close interaction with you is particularly important in this regard. Together, we achieve wind successes and secure the energy supply system of the future.

Whether it’s individual projects, up-front costs in connection with the acquisition of an existing wind farm eligible for repowering, or long-term partnerships: Almost everything is possible in a partnership with RWE Renewables Europe & Australia.

Securing a site

We operate our own wind farms, plan for our own portfolio, and focus on a long-term, fair partnership with site owners. That creates benefits for us that are crucial in the early stages of project development in particular.

Approval process

In our partnerships, we look after the material development budget. That leaves you free to concentrate on the project development aspect. We have many years of experience with complex approval processes and application procedures.

Call for tenders

Confidence in a market setting: We excel in a competitive environment, and our efficient planning and market analysis enable us to steer wind projects successfully through the tendering process.

Financing

When it comes to financing joint wind projects, RWE Renewables Europe & Australia operates independently at a high level and offers attractive financing opportunities that have been reviewed by experts.

Purchasing

As a major company with an international ambit, RWE Renewables Europe & Australia achieves excellent purchasing conditions for wind turbines, covering the wind power infrastructure, other components and services. The result is a level of competitiveness that our partners will also benefit from.

Construction

RWE Renewables Europe & Australia has acquired as much experience in the construction of new wind farms as it’s possible to have. We pool our skills for every new project and apply the experience we have acquired in construction projects on complex sites.

Operation

Whenever the wind blows, our state-of-the-art control rooms record it in the system, 24/7. With power in real time, RWE Renewables Europe & Australia achieves the best possible marketing results and levels of availability. And at a technical level, plant operation couldn’t be in better hands in terms of servicing and maintenance.
