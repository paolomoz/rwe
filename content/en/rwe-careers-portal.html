RWE in the Americas

Across the Americas, our teams support energy projects from development through to operation. Through wind, solar and battery storage projects, as well as flexible generation, we contribute to the energy transition. Working closely with communities, partners and local organisations, we deliver projects that create lasting value for the regions in which we operate.

About RWE in the Americas

RWE in Australia

Australia is an important growth market for RWE. Our onshore wind, solar and battery storage projects are developed in close collaboration with local communities and Traditional Owners, balancing environmental, cultural and regional interests.

About RWE in Australia

RWE in the Benelux

Across the Benelux region, RWE combines long-standing energy expertise with innovative technologies for the future. Our teams work across renewable energy, flexible generation, battery storage and hydrogen while helping to develop existing energy infrastructure in partnership with businesses, governments and local organisations.

About RWE in the Benelux

RWE in China

From China, our teams support RWE's activities across Asia. They work in areas including energy trading, technical advisory and project management, strengthening collaboration with partners and customers throughout the region.

About RWE in China

RWE in Denmark

Denmark plays a key role in our offshore wind activities. Together with our partners, our teams are helping develop renewable energy projects while supporting the sustainable development of tomorrow's energy system.

RWE in Germany (headquarter)

Germany is home to RWE. From our headquarters in Essen and many other locations, including Hamburg, our teams work across a wide range of business areas – from renewable and conventional energy to energy trading, research, development and recultivation. Together, we are shaping the transformation of the energy system while taking responsibility for the regions where we operate.

Learn more about RWE

RWE in France

In France, open dialogue with local authorities, partners and communities is an integral part of every project. Alongside the development, construction and operation of onshore wind and solar projects, this helps build trust and supports a more sustainable energy future.

About RWE in France

RWE in Greece

Together with our partner HELLENiQ Energy, RWE is developing solar projects in Greece and supporting the development of offshore wind. Working closely with partners and local communities, our teams help advance the energy transition while creating lasting value for the regions where we operate.

RWE in India

From our Mumbai office, our teams work closely with colleagues around the world. As part of RWE Supply & Trading, they support international trading activities while connecting global markets with regional expertise.

RWE in Indonesia

From our Jakarta office, RWE Supply & Trading brings together its commodity trading and marketing activities in Indonesia. Our teams work across Commercial Trading, Business Analysis, Finance and Corporate Reporting, supporting the continued development of our business in Indonesia.

In Ireland, RWE develops onshore and offshore wind projects as well as battery storage. Working closely with communities and partners, our teams help drive the energy transition. Community Benefit Funds support local initiatives and contribute to the long-term development of the areas where we operate.

RWE in Italy

Across Italy, RWE develops, builds and operates onshore wind and solar projects. Working together with local authorities, partners and organisations, we create solutions that support the energy transition while delivering long-term value for local communities.

About RWE in Italy

RWE in Japan

In Japan, RWE combines renewable energy development with energy trading and innovative decarbonisation solutions. Together with our partners, our teams work on offshore wind projects, power purchase agreements (PPAs) and energy trading activities to support the country's energy transition.

About RWE in Japan

RWE in Poland

In Poland, RWE is expanding its onshore wind and solar portfolio. Together with local authorities, regional businesses and partners, we contribute to the energy transition while supporting the long-term development of the regions where we operate.

About RWE in Poland

RWE in Singapore

Singapore is an important location for RWE Supply & Trading in the Asia-Pacific region. From here, our teams operate across international energy and commodity markets, connecting regional activities with RWE's global business.

RWE in Spain

In Spain, RWE develops, builds and operates onshore wind and solar projects throughout their entire lifecycle. Working closely with local authorities, partners and communities, we help advance the energy transition while supporting a more sustainable energy supply.

RWE in the United Kingdom

The UK is one of RWE's most diverse energy markets. Our teams work across offshore and onshore wind, solar, hydropower, biomass, gas, battery storage and hydrogen. Through Community Funds and initiatives focused on education, skills, biodiversity and local partnerships, we are committed to creating lasting value for the communities where we operate.

About RWE in the United Kingdom

Where and how can I apply?

You can find all advertised positions on our job portal. If a position is of interest to you, please apply only via our online application platform, which you can access from any job description by clicking on the ‘Apply now’ button.

Can I submit an unsolicited application to RWE?

Unfortunately, it is not possible to submit an unsolicited application at RWE, as our recruiting is a decentralised responsibility of the individual RWE operational companies. There is no central office within the Group that can check unsolicited applications and forward them to relevant departments - and also guarantee you a good application process. We hope you will nevertheless find what you are looking for in our job board. Thank you for your understanding.

What formats and file sizes are permitted for uploading documents?

You can upload files in .doc, .txt, .ppt, .pdf, .tif, .gif, .bmp, .jpg and .html formats.

We prefer PDFs. We regret that file formats not listed here, including .zip files, cannot be processed. The attachments must not exceed a total size of 5 MB.

Whom can I contact if I have any questions?

The relevant contacts for each job offer can be found directly in the respective job description. If you have questions regarding a specific position, it is best to reach out to the contacts listed there. You can also find answers to many frequently asked questions on our dedicated FAQ page.

The contact filter will help you find further HR contacts, for example for specific programmes. Use this filter to locate contacts for particular areas of your interest.

For general questions, suggestions, or feedback regarding our career websites, a dedicated contact is available. Please note that this contact is not intended for recruitment enquiries, questions about the status of your application, or individual roles.
