Sandra Dettmer is joining the Management Board of RWE Renewables Europe & Australia

style
article-stage

  • Experienced energy manager to take up newly created position of Chief Operating Officer (COO) at RWE Renewables Europe & Australia on 1 April 2026
  • Responsibilities include Engineering, Construction and Operation of wind and solar plants and battery storages, as well as HSE (Health, Safety, Environment)

Essen, 11 March 2026

Sandra Dettmer will take on the role of Chief Operating Officer (COO) on the Management Board of RWE Renewables Europe & Australia GmbH on 1 April. This move will enable RWE to place even greater management focus on the growing number of onshore wind, solar and battery storage projects under construction and in operation in Europe and Australia. Dettmer's future responsibilities at RWE Renewables Europe & Australia will include Engineering, Construction and Operation of wind, solar and battery parks, as well as HSE. Sandra Dettmer joins CEO Sopna Sury and CFO Simon Bockmühl on the management board. Dettmer was most recently Director of Construction at RWE Renewables Europe & Australia.

Markus Krebber, CEO of RWE AG: “In Sandra Dettmer, we have a highly experienced energy manager from within our own ranks. She is very familiar with our renewables business in Europe and Australia and will successfully continue the safe operation and rapid construction of our onshore wind, solar and battery parks. These technologies are an important cornerstone of RWE's value-driven growth. I am very much looking forward to working with Sandra in the future.”
