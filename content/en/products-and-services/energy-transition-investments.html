RWE Energy Transition Investments (“RWE ETI”), established in 2011 as RWE Principal Investments, is an investment vehicle of RWE Supply & Trading GmbH with a mandate to manage a diverse portfolio of Private Equity type asset investments and company shareholdings. RWE ETI’s focus is on achieving attractive returns on the capital deployed whilst also ensuring new investments are consistent with RWE’s strategic goals and supportive of the global energy transition. Investments are intended to be complementary to, rather than competing with the renewables’ core business. The team is open to working with suitable co-investors on transactions, and has done so successfully on multiple previous investments.

With a successful history of diverse investments across the energy landscape, the RWE Energy Transition Investments team will continue to leverage its extensive transaction and international energy markets expertise to explore new opportunities that are aligned with RWE’s target of carbon neutrality by 2040.

Portfolio

The team manages a diverse portfolio of existing investments in companies and assets whose value is correlated to the value of the commodity that the company or asset produces / trades / transports. Recent and future investments are concentrated on business areas expected to prosper based on analysis and understanding of how energy markets will develop through the energy transition.

This following section is for information purposes only and nothing in this site is intended to be relied upon. The investments identified are not representative of all the investments purchased or sold and no statement is made or to be implied as to the affairs and profitability of the portfolio companies. The holding structure for investments depends on the nature of the portfolio companies.

Our investment experts

A team of highly experienced investment experts split between RWE offices in London and New York. This ensures effective interaction with the geographically diverse portfolio companies, whilst allowing the team to maximise synergies with RWE’s core business wherever possible.

The diversity and combined experience within the Energy Transition Investments team, across Private Equity, Investment Banking and Commodity Trading, creates an atmosphere in which an extraordinary range of global energy investment opportunities can be effectively evaluated, executed and managed.
