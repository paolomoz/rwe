RWE produces hydrogen from renewables by means of electrolysis

RWE uses electricity from its own solar and wind power plants to produce 100% climate-neutral and thus green hydrogen. During (water) electrolysis, water is split into hydrogen and oxygen with the help of electricity. RWE will make the hydrogen available via various channels.

Delivery via filling facilities

RWE is planning to supply hydrogen via filling facilities where hydrogen trailers can be filled with green, certified hydrogen. The trailers can transport hydrogen directly to the customer across long distances.

Delivery via hydrogen filling stations

Both municipal vehicles, such as buses or refuse collection vehicles, and private cars can refuel with green hydrogen from RWE at public hydrogen filling stations. For more information, visit our joint venture two4H2 with Westfalen AG.

Delivery via pipeline

Hydrogen will be supplied through emerging pipeline networks such as H2ercules. The pipeline between Lingen, Marl, and Gelsenkirchen-Scholven will become part of the hydrogen core network. In the long term, RWE customers throughout Germany will be able to be supplied with high-quality hydrogen over long distances.

Achieving the company’s goal of becoming climate neutral

Achieving the company’s goal of becoming climate neutral

For medium-sized and large companies alike, hydrogen can play an essential role on the road towards greater sustainability. When a company sets itself ambitious targets for reducing emissions and communicates these to its stakeholders, strategic action is required. With green hydrogen as a sustainable energy source for industry and transport, companies can reduce their carbon footprint in the long term. This also has a direct impact on ESG rankings and expenditure on CO2 certificates in emissions trading, of course.
