All aspects of the economic adaptation of an existing power plant park to the challenges posed by the growing share of renewable generation had to be managed within our own large generation portfolio.

We cover valuable expertise in thermal generation and operational topics.

We cover:

  • flexibility
  • low load operation
  • fuel switch (e.g. from coal to biomass)
  • process safety
  • emission reduction

Looking ahead to the future and the energy transition, battery storages and hydrogen are also important issues in which RWE is supporting several clients. We also offer remote support, (online) trainings/webinars and assist with smart glasses to ensure that the projects are successfully achieved.

We have gained many decades of experience in mining. Here, too, we are proud of the fact that we have successfully offered added value to our many international customers in their open-pit mining operations (especially iron ore, copper, coal and many other products). We are currently focusing on recultivation, green mining, mine operations, material handling and IPCC support.
