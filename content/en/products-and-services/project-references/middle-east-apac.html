In the region Middle East and Asia-Pacific (APAC) our experts provide consulting services in the fields of mining, thermal generation and renewables.

From planning, construction to operation we support clients during all project stages. With regard to mining topics, RWE is currently focusing on recultivation, green mining, mine operation, materials handling and IPCC support.

Furthermore, we cover thermal generation and operational topics in this region such as flexibility, low load operation, fuel switch (e.g. from coal to biomass), process safety and emission reduction.

Looking ahead to the future and the energy transition, battery storages and hydrogen are also important issues in which RWE is supporting several clients. We also offer remote support, (online) trainings/webinars and assist with smart glasses to ensure that the projects are successfully achieved.

RWE has vast experience based on our national and international projects, which we will apply to the benefit of our customers.
