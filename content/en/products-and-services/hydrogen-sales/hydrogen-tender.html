With the commissioning of a hydrogen filling facility in Lingen, RWE is starting to sell the first quantities of certified* green hydrogen. The earliest possible start of procurement is the second quarter of 2025. Hydrogen quantities will be released iteratively as the production facilities are being ramped up and the hydrogen is becoming available. Further amounts of hydrogen will be made available to the market in bilateral contract negotiations.

Interested parties can get in touch with our contact persons at any time.

* Certification will be carried out once all regulatory requirements from the EU Commission have been publicised.
