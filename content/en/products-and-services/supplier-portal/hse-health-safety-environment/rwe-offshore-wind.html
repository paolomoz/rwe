Our 3 Care Principles

To build a stable wind turbine there is more to it than technical skills.

The generator that drives us is our Culture of Care to act in a healthy and safe way.

The blades symbolise our 3 We Care Principles:

style
cp-prose

1. CARE STARTS WITH ME
I take ownership, speak up when I feel work is affecting health & safety negatively and have the courage to share failures and lessons learned.

2. WE CARE AS A TEAM
We are brave in our decisions and talk to each other when we feel health or safety is at risk, we take care of each other and can reduce sources of pressure as a team.

3. WE LEAD TO CARE
Our leaders practice what they preach by being a role model and in setting up a safe and healthy structures and procedures that constantly improve.
