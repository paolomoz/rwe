Welcome

You would like to become a new supplier of us and would like to support us in fulfilling our tasks being one of the biggest international multi-utility companies?
If this is the case, this is the place to be!

Why register?

By registering you give us the chance to notice you. The registration helps you to find the right contact person in our procurement department and saves you further queries.

How is it done?

You find a contact form below which will help you to specify your product range.

What’s happening then?

Your query is automatically transferred to the contact person and product specialist who - if interested - will contact you. So please be so kind not to contact us by phone if you have a query.

Thank you for your co-operation.
