Health | Safety | Environment

RWE promotes a culture of care in everything we do in order to prevent harm to people, assets, environment, society and our planet.

RWE has implemented a number of health, safety and environmental (HSE) measures to drive HSE excellence.

We are committed, at all levels in the organization and together with business partners within our supply chain, to foster a safe and healthy work culture and work conditions, to protect the environment and to constantly strive for long-term and sustainable performance so that we can all #EnjoyTomorrow.
