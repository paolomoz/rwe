On the right track with transparency and openness

Selected suppliers are objectively evaluated by RWE-internal experts. Uniform evaluation criteria are used throughout the Group, focussing on the following central aspects:

We work fast and paperless via the internet, providing you with easy access to your assessment by RWE at any time.

Thus, the successful performance of the best suppliers, but also the missing performance of poorly rated suppliers are communicated in a transparent and uniform way.

The best-performing suppliers take centre stage due to uniform evaluation criteria and the RWE-wide communication of supplier evaluation results. These results form the basis for the agreement of specific improvement objectives between RWE and its suppliers.
