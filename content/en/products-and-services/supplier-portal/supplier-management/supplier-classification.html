Identification and consistent implementation of individual approaches

The category to which a supplier is assigned is determined by combining the evaluation of suppliers with the assessment of their strategic status, which is established on the basis of uniform and objective criteria.

This classification forms the basis for optimising the cooperation between the supplier and RWE. The supplier has the possibility to move into a higher-rated category by improving its performance and aligning its strategy with RWE requirements. This will enhance the supplier’s opportunities for establishing business relations.
