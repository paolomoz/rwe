Lignite Energy – An economic and high-quality industrial fuel

LE – Lignite Energy

style
article-stage

LE Lignite Energy is a low-cost and high-quality industrial fuel. It is obtained by drying and grinding up lignite. The stable low price of the basic raw material and its ready availability make LE a cost-effective alternative to gas, oil and hard coal. LE is a reliable long-term source of energy, and RBB can deliver consistently high quality at a stable price, making fluctuations in energy prices a thing of the past. Energy-intensive industries such as the asphalt, cement and lime sectors can make the most of favourable delivery conditions. However, the fuel can also be used for a number of specialist applications, including for steam generation, thanks to its broad range of applications. Discover the economic and technological advantages Lignite Energy can offer in your industry!
