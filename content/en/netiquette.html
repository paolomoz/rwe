We use our social media channels to give insights into the energy world, to publish news and showcase interesting facts about RWE and the people who work with us. While discussions and dialogue are the life-blood of social media, we want to ensure that the tone on our social media channels stays respectful.

As a result, we ask you to follow these rules:

We want communication to be constructive and based on respect and fairness. We will not tolerate verbal abuse and insults.

We do not offer a platform for inappropriate or offensive posts.

Accordingly, we reserve the right to hide posts that do not follow these netiquette rules by hiding, deleting or reporting posts, and block accounts if there are frequent or major infringements involving the following issues:

  • Insults, verbal abuse, degrading comments
  • Inflammatory, racist, obscene, discriminatory, pornographic content
  • Illegal content, e.g. incitement or instructions to commit crimes, and content that violates the copyright or data privacy of third parties
  • False, fraudulent or misleading content
  • Links to external websites, spam content
  • Names and contact details of our employees, confidential contact details
  • Content associated with viruses, Trojans or harmful websites, or otherwise capable of impairing the usability of our channels

Moreover, the Terms of Service of the respective social network apply.

With this in mind, we look forward to open and fair communication.
