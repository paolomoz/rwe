Title
The company history of RWE
Description
Over 125 years of electrifying history. Find out about the history of RWE - from the founding of the Rheinisch-Westfälischen Elektrizitätswerk in 1898 until today.
Theme
solid-header

This is how RWE’s 125-year history can be summarised in a nutshell. It all began in April 1898 with the founding of the Rheinisch-Westfälische Elektrizitätswerk as a public company in Essen, Germany. At the end of the 19th century, the use of electricity in agriculture, industry and private households was practically non-existent. However, as RWE’s growth took off, electricity became a big seller that brought prosperity and progress to many generations of people.

Already a pioneer of a growing industrial society back in the day, the Group is now driving the international green energy transition.

Immerse yourself in RWE’s electrifying corporate history.
