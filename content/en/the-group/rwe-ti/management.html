Introducing the management team of RWE Technology International

RWE Technology International has developed into one of the world's leading project management and engineering companies for renewable energies, efficient mining, conventional generation, and grid stabilisation.

The company is represented by managing directors Götz Hanau and Natalie Henßen.

The Management Board is responsible in particular for corporate management, business policy and corporate planning.

Götz Hanau

Managing Director Technology Solutions & Project Execution

As Managing Director “Technical Solutions & Project Execution” at RWE Technology International, Götz is responsible for the technical planning, development and implementation of all RWE asset investments for security of supply and flexibility. He is also in charge of central engineering, project procurement and system and governance development.

Natalie Henßen

Managing Director Global Solutions & Corporate Services
