Implement waste heat recovery

Wepa Paper mill (Netherlands): Energy saving concept development by evalutating site utilities, heat consumption and recycle residual heat from the production process.

Bavaria Brewery Lieshout (Netherlands): Energy saving concept development by evalutating site utilities, heat consumption and recycle residual heat from the production process.

Improve enviromental pollution control

RWE Group (Germany / UK): Technical project management for various fossil-fuel plants including review plant equipment, analysis potential improvement areas, selection of suitable measures and implementation support  (CO2, SO2 and NOx).

VALE (Brazil): Specialist consulting services for emission control mitigation of bulk material handling equipment in port/stockpile operation and advice on optimization of flue gas emissions in pelletizing plants.

Croatia: Identify plant measures to comply with new emission legislation incl. plant assessment, potential improvement areas, selection of suitable measures and cofiring of RDF.

Reduce auxiliary power requirements

Eemshaven Holding BV/ RWE Generation (Netherlands): Implementation of sootblowers for the hot steam return to the feed water tank to reduce auxiliary power requirements.

Improve turbine cycle

China Light & Power (CLP) (Hong Kong): Acting as project manager and operation and process engineer to upgrade gas turbine compressor, cycle combustion system and thermodynamic cycle to reduce NOX Emission and increase efficiency.

Improve boiler efficiency

Malakoff (Malaysia): Support in boiler design, fuel management and plant optimization to improve thermal and energy efficiency.

Co-firing/Fuel switch

RWE Group (Germany/Netherlands/UK): Acting as owner's engineer we project managed development, from design and construction supervision to commission and take over for various major biomass cofiring & fuel switch projects - namely hard-coal fired Amer/Eemshaven (NL) and Tilbury (UK).

United Arab Emirates: Technical review of feasibility study aiming to evaluate camel dung utilization for biogas or combustion plant.

Germany / UK: Feasiblity study for a high efficient mono combustion plant firing residual sludge, including basic design, site selection, technology selection and cost estimate.

Turbine retrofit

Steag (Germany): Turbine retrofit at hard-coal fired power plant Bergkamen (1 x 800 MW) including performance measurement of steam turbine.

RWE Power AG (Germany): Steam turbine retrofit in lignite fired power plant Weisweiler (2 x 600 MW), including performance measurement of steam turbine.

Mátrai Erőmű ZRt (Hungary): Acting as owner's engineer we supported the installation of topping gas turbines in lignite fired power plant Matra (2 x 25 MW GT, 1 x 300 MW ST).
