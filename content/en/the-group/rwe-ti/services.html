Mining Consulting

We have gained extensive experience and expertise in the mining sector, which means that we can provide you with individual advice on any type of opencast mining. Our portfolio of services is diverse, covering the entire mining cycle. From planning to commissioning, operation as well as optimisation and rehabilitation to mine closure, we assist you with our expertise. We also advise you on aspects of environmental management in order to minimise environmental impacts and emissions.

Mining machinery and equipment

In the field of machinery and equipment, our many years of experience enable us to offer you a wide range of service packages, including crushing equipment (including our own laboratories) and IPCC. This means that we can provide you with competent advice on any mining or bulk material transport problem and develop appropriate concept studies. In addition to this, we offer training on topics relevant to opencast mining, such as geotechnical engineering, maintenance, safety or management.
