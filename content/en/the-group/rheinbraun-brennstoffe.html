RV Rheinbraun Handel und Dienstleistungen GmbH 

RV Rheinbraun Handel und Dienstleistungen GmbH is the management company for the Refinement business of RWE Power. In addition to producing refined lignite products, the Refinement business area also includes the sale and logistics of various types of solid fuels.

Rheinbraun Brennstoff GmbH 

Rheinbraun Brennstoff GmbH is a wholly owned subsidiary of Rheinbraun Handel und Dienstleistungen GmbH. Here, fuels made by RWE Power are sold to industrial and private customers along with retail products to round off our range. The three production sites are all found in the Rhenish coalfields; the products are sold throughout Europe.
