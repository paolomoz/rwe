RWE AG
Description
RWE AG is a leader in the generation and trading of electricity from conventional and renewable energy sources. The group employs around 20,000 people worldwide and has a clear target: to get to net zero by 2040. This is done in close cooperation with the subsidiaries RWE Renewables Europe & Australia, RWE Offshore Wind, RWE Americas, RWE Generation, RWE Supply & Trading and RWE Power.

An overview of the company

Our society and the energy that we rely on are constantly changing. Digitalisation and electrification are rapidly reshaping the world we live in. At the same time, the demand for clean and secure electricity is growing. The successful transformation of our energy supply is the key to a sustainable future and RWE is resolutely driving that change.

We have opened a new chapter in the history of our company. We are seizing the opportunities presented by these changes by first changing ourselves. We now have a clear focus on generating electricity, building new storage capacity and trading our energy. We are growing internationally with renewables and breaking into new markets.

The new RWE has been one of the leading international providers of renewable energies from day one. We are already number two in the world for offshore wind power. We have a determined focus on growth and are investing heavily in the expansion of renewables and storage technologies. With our flexible wind power fleet, we strengthen the nation’s security of supply. We are putting our energy to use for a sustainable future. Our activities centre on the subsidiaries RWE Renewables Europe & Australia GmbH, RWE Offshore Wind, RWE Americas, RWE Generation, RWE Power, and RWE Supply & Trading.
