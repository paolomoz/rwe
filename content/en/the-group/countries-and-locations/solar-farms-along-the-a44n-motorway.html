Sustainable transformation in the Rhenish lignite area, North Rhine-Westphalia

Solar farms along the A44n motorway

style
article-stage

RWE operates large solar plants along the A44n motorway in the Rhineland. The plants of the first phase are located on recultivated land between the municipalities Bedburg and Jüchen. With a total capacity of 86.5 MWp (74.6 MWac), around 141,000 solar modules supply climate-friendly electricity to approximately 27,700 households since December 2025. These projects mark an important step in the structural change and strengthen the sustainable development of the region.

The solar plants along the A44n are not only a model for further projects, but also a symbol of the transition to renewable energy solutions. With this project, RWE is once again demonstrating how former opencast mining sites can be effectively repurposed for the future.

Sheep at work: nature-friendly grass management in RWE’s solar parks

RWE is relying on animal assistance to maintain its solar park: a regional flock of over 1,000 ewes and lambs will take over the grass management between the solar panels in the summer of 2026. A successful pilot project at the RWE solar plant in Bedburg has thus become standard practice.

Implementation of the second extension phase, with a capacity of 19.9 MW peak (15.5 MWac), is planned for 2026. The expansion will comprise more than 30,600 solar modules and will take shape on recultivated land at the Garzweiler opencast mine in the municipality of Jüchen.
