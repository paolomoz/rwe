The onshore wind farm Königshovener Höhe was installed on recultivated site of open cast mine Garzweiler and is a cooperation project between RWE (51%) and the municipality of Bedburg (49%). The wind farm has a total capacity of 67 megawatts. The 21 wind turbines can generate enough green electricity to cover the annual demand of up to 58,000 households.

In its more than 20 years of experience in the development, construction and operation of wind farms, RWE also relies on local partnerships. In the meantime, RWE has successfully implemented several wind farms with the participation of municipalities, municipal companies and citizens.

Status: In operation
