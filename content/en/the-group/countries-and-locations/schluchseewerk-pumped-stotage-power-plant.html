Biggest long-term storage facility in Germany

Schluchseewerk AG, a 50% subsidiary of RWE Power, operates five pumped-storage power plants in the Black Forest with a total installed capacity of 1,800 MW. As contractually agreed, RWE Power can dispatch half of the installed generating sets and, hence, 50% of the installed capacity of Schluchseewerk AG.
