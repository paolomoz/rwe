Nordsee One offshore wind farm is located north of the island of Juist. The 54 wind turbines of the wind farm, which are having a water depth of 25 to 29 metres, have a total capacity of 332 megawatts. The wind farm produces enough green electricity each year to supply approximately 400,000 households.

The wind farm is owned by Northland Power from Canada (85 per cent) and RWE (15 per cent).

Status: In operation
