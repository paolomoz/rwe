RWE Supply & Trading Asia-Pacific Pte Ltd. (“RWEST AP”), a wholly owned RWE Supply & Trading (“RWEST”) subsidiary was incorporated in 2010 and has since developed into the Asian headquarters of RWEST, with subsidiaries in China and Indonesia.

Based in Singapore, one of the most vibrant countries of the world, RWEST AP has become a pillar of RWEST’s growth ambitions in Asia.

RWEST AP is an active player in the Asian and global physical and financial wholesale commodity markets.
