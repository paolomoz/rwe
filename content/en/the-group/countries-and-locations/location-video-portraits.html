Rødsand 2 Offshore Wind Farm

Our Rødsand 2 Offshore Wind Farm is located in the Danish part of the Baltic Sea. With its 90 wind turbines and an installed capacity of 207 MW, the wind farm can supply the equivalent of 200,000 Danish households with renewable energy.

Onshore Wind Farm Zuidwester

As a part of Europe’s largest wind farm, Noordoostpolder, the Zuidwester Onshore Wind Farm consists of twelve state-of-the-art wind turbines. Each of these turbines has a rotor diameter of 126 metres and an output of 7.5 MW.

Herdecke Pumped-storage Power Plant

Our Herdecke Pumped-storage Power Plant enables us to store electricity and feed it back into the grid when needed. The pumped-storage power plant acts like a battery for the electricity grid, storing surplus electricity in the form of ‘altitude metres’.

Stephenstown Battery Storage Facility

The Stephenstown Battery Storage Facility is RWE’s first battery storage project in Europe. The battery storage facility in Balbriggan, Ireland, was commissioned in 2021 and ensures a smooth flow of electricity into the grid as Ireland continues its transition to renewable energy.
