Inclusive working conditions

RWE projects and initiatives that foster inclusion and drive change on a global and local scale

Sometimes, it's the little things that really count. Every small change adds up to the big picture. Whether it's our commitment to flexible working arrangements, supporting people as they become parents, or getting rid of biases from our systems, we take a full, holistic approach, knowing that subtle changes can make a meaningful difference.

style
cp-prose

We understand that everyone has different out-of-work and personal circumstances, and we endeavour to support regardless of what these are. That’s why flexible and mobile working are options which can be available such as parent-child offices and in-house day nurseries on some sites, part-time working, hybrid-arrangements, and flexible hours.

In certain areas of RWE, we offer special coaching for parents and families looking to make the best decisions with regards to parental leave and returning to work afterwards. We have very experienced suppliers in the field of parental leave coaching on hand, meaning our approach even further supports employees.

We strive to be as reasonably accessible as possible. This, therefore, includes making changes on our campuses to make sure no employee is limited or held back at work. We don’t forget that it’s the small details that make a difference, from ensuring wheelchairs can move freely in our canteens to including a kindergarten on our Essen campus, and providing contemplation rooms for religious practice at some of our sites.

The Group Representative for Severely Disabled Employees (KSV), works alongside the Inclusion Officers and Social Counsellors to advocate for the interests of employees in Germany with health restrictions and actively support the creation of an equal opportunity working environment.
