Energy expertise for a reliable and sustainable future
Our products and services for partners worldwide

RWE Supply & Trading is the interface between RWE and the world's energy markets and operates as a center of excellence for energy trading and procurement within the Group. As a global market player, we not only connect energy generation, trading, and supply, but also offer a broad portfolio of products and services for utilities, industrial companies, and business partners worldwide.

Our offering includes electricity and gas supply and trading, price hedging and portfolio management, as well as innovative green energy solutions – from power purchase agreements (PPAs) and guarantees of origin to tailor-made trading strategies.

With in-depth market knowledge, an international presence and decades of experience, our teams of specialists develop solutions that combine security, flexibility and sustainability. Whether physical energy supply, structured procurement, or risk hedging, RWE Supply & Trading supports you in exploiting opportunities in dynamic energy markets and strengthening your long-term competitiveness.

Discover our global product and service portfolio – for a secure, transparent, and sustainable energy future.
