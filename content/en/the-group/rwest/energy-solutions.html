Our Expertise

With a rich history spanning over 125 years, we are committed to expanding our investments in renewable energy and storage technologies. Our adaptable fleet of power plants plays a crucial role in ensuring the nation’s energy security. As the demand for clean and secure electricity increases, the successful transformation of our energy supply is essential for a sustainable future and we are dedicated to driving this change.

We provide energy and energy-related services to large industrial customers, municipalities and aggregators. Our offerings include standard supply, trading products, and customized solutions. Through exceptional asset and portfolio management, we create mutually beneficial outcomes.
