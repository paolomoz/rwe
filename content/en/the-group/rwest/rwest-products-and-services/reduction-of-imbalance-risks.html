Our approach

Easy set-up of online measuring instruments: If required, we can equip your measuring points with the necessary controllers and ensure reliable transmission of measured values.

Continuous automatic forecasting of your load profile/portfolio load profile: Based on the online measured values and using data science methods, the appropriate algorithm continuously forecasts your consumption or generation within a valid confidence interval, usually per minute and for the short-term.

Calculation of deviation or position: The forecast data is compared with your initial day-ahead nomination or your net book position that was transmitted. The deviation determined is fed into the intraday trading process for active portfolio/balancing group management.
