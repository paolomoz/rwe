Become a balancing group pool participant

To maximise your benefit from continuous market participation through automated trading on the intraday market, you should enter into the RWE Supply & Trading (RWEST) balancing group cooperation.

Jointly defined trading strategy

We will jointly develop a detailed trading strategy tailored to your portfolio, the current market situation and your risk requirements.

Automate trading

The trading strategy is automated around the clock (24/7) using coded algorithms that take into account your daily forecasting and distribution load profiles. This ensures you are able to take action at any time, when the situation requires it.

Full transparency

Daily, monthly and annual analysis provides you with full transparency on the performance of your portfolio.
