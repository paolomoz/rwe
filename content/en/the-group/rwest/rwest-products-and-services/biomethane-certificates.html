About ISCC

ISCC – International Sustainability and Carbon Certification –  provides a globally applicable certification system for the sustainability of raw materials and products, traceability through the supply chain and the determination of greenhouse gas emissions and savings. ISCC certification scheme is recognized by the European Commission ass compliant with REDII.
