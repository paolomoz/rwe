RWE Green Heat Plus

Decide now for RWEGreenHeatPlus and secure your steam and heat supply of tomorrow.

Is your production running at full power, requiring a lot of process heat that has to be sourced cost-effectively whenever you need it? Fossil fuel prices are becoming more and more unpredictable. Thus, you are uncertain about your future strategy for heat supply?

One thing is for sure: the energy market is in a state of change!

Carbon-neutral heat production will become mandatory at some point in the future.

Fossil energy sources such as gas can no longer be provided in unlimited quantities. At the same time, they are subject to ever greater fluctuations. These challenges require alternatives: the electrification of your heat production through electrode boilers.

style
cp-prose, grey, plain

We've got just the right solution here : RWE Green Heat Plus.

With RWE Green Heat Plus we offer you a modular service package around the electrode boiler - the ideal solution for keeping your business supplied with sustainable heat.

style
cp-prose

We put your needs first: at your request, we will plan, deliver and install your new electrode boiler on site and can even run the system for you.

style
cp-prose

Our product portfolio also includes the possible supply of your electrode boiler with green electricity based on a long-term supply contract. Your carbon-free heat and steam supply is therefore guaranteed - today and in the future. At the same time, you make an important contribution to the energy and heat transition.

style
cp-prose

In addition, with our Fuel Switch Optimisation strategy, you can combine the electrode boiler with your existing gas boiler or CHP plant to create a hybrid system that is flexible and enables you to use the most cost-efficient energy source at each moment.

style
cp-prose

And that’s not all: with the electrode boiler, you can participate in the balancing energy market. This generates additional revenue for your company and contributes to a quick return on investment. And when your electrode boiler is producing during periods when more electricity is being fed into the grid, you'll be helping to integrate renewables and stabilise the electricity grid.
