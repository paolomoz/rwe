Products

D-Central enables you to become part of our virtual power plant

Decentralised generation plants and flexible loads are becoming key to meeting the growing demands of the balancing power market.

Balancing reserve is designed to compensate fluctuations in the power grid in order to keep the grid frequency stable. This balancing can either be positive or negative. D-Central is a solution that allows you to market technical units depending on your capabilities, whether they are disconnectable loads or generation units.

style
cp-prose
Frequency containment reserve

Do you own a plant with at least 1 MW capacity? Then you can benefit from the provision of primary reserve capacity in weekly tenders.

Automatic frequency restoration reserve

Have your available power from ± 5 MW automatically accessed by the flexibility markets and thus benefit from the secondary reserve.

Manual frequency restoration

You want to participate in daily tenders with your plant and have 5 MW or more available? Market this for the minute reserve.

Intraday

D-Central also offers access to intraday trading. This gives you the opportunity to profit from the volatility of electricity markets in the short term and on a continuous basis.

Capacity reserve

You have the capacity to provide capacity reserves? D-Central allows you to benefit from these flexibilities.

Disconnectable loads

You operate loads of at least 5 MW, which can be switched off and thus flexibly used? D-Central enables you to generate profit from these loads.
