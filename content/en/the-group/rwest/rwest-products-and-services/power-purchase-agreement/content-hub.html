PPAs from operational assets

In the UK, RWE has a significant presence being one of the largest electricity generators. With a vast multi-technology operational asset fleet and a strong pipeline of development projects, we’re leading the renewable energy transition and fostering economic resilience and well-being in the regions where we operate.

The UK Commodity Solutions Team can offer various tenors and pricing structures, and our agreements can be fully tailored to meet your specific needs, whether you require integration with or without your electricity supplier.
