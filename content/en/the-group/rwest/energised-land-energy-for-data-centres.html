‘energised Land’ – Energy for data centres

RWE enables high-performance, sustainable data centres in strategic industrial locations

Powering the Future of data centres

A Trusted Partner for Europe’s Digital Growth

Digitalisation and AI are transforming the global economy, driving unprecedented demand for scalable, resilient, and sustainable data centre infrastructure. Developers face increasing complexity in securing land, grid access, and long-term clean power at the required speed.

With more than 125 years of energy-sector expertise, a leading position in renewable generation, and strong local connections, RWE is uniquely positioned to accelerate the deployment of next-generation data centres across Europe.

With energised Land, RWE provides the foundation for the successful development of large-scale data centres—combining industrial sites with critical infrastructure and integrated energy solutions.

Additional services include asset development, water supply, cooling concepts, energy management (including 24/7 green power and direct lines), and waste heat utilisation.

Green Power Supply

Green Power Supply

As one of Europe’s largest producers of renewable energy, RWE provides data centres with secure, affordable, and sustainable power.

Our tailored Power Purchase Agreements (PPAs) offer:

  • Guaranteed long‑term renewable supply
  • Price stability and risk mitigation
  • Direct pathways to net‑zero and ESG compliance
Site-specific solutions

Site-specific solutions

To support resilience and flexibility, RWE develops on‑site, behind‑the‑meter energy assets for data‑centre partners, including:

  • Gas/H2 peakers
  • Battery storage systems
  • Hybrid backup concepts
  • Bridge‑to‑grid solutions enabling earlier operational start

These solutions reduce reliance on diesel generators, increase efficiency, and unlock additional value through participation in energy markets.
We own and manage secure, grid-connected power generation facilities on-site to accelerate the deployment of data centres.
