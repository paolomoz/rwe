RWE AI Research Laboratory

The RWE AI Research Laboratory was established in 2024 in Bellevue, WA, part of the Greater Seattle Area.

We aim to become a global leader in developing forecasting models using advanced Artificial Intelligence (AI) within a specialist research project.

Key features

Global Weather ForecastsProviding comprehensive forecasts for all relevant weather variables, including temperature, precipitation, and wind.

Renewable ForecastingSpecialists focus on optimizing RWE's global portfolio through advanced renewable energy forecasting.

Probabilistic ForecastingOur primary objective is to become a global leader in probabilistic forecasting using advanced generative AI models trained on large and diverse datasets, including proprietary data captured at source.
