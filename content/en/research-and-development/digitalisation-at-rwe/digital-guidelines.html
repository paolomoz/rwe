Our AI Guidelines: Where integrity meets innovation

style
cp-prose
  • Human-Centricity
    The use of AI at RWE is based on fundamental values, human rights, and the well-being of all stakeholders. AI systems are always controlled and supervised.
  • Transparency & Explainability
    We ensure that the purpose, application, and results of our AI are comprehensible, understandable, and communicated openly.
  • Reliability, Safety & Quality
    Through ongoing testing, high-quality data, and continuous monitoring, RWE guarantees a precise, resilient, and secure AI infrastructure.
  • Diversity, Equity & Inclusion
    RWE promotes equal opportunities and actively prevents discrimination. AI applications are guided by values such as fairness, diversity, and our Code of Conduct.
  • Data Protection & Cybersecurity
    Our AI-driven processes comply with legal requirements, follow a “privacy/security by design” approach, and reliably protect personal data at all times.
  • Proportionality
    Our AI approach is risk-based to ensure that efficiency and innovation align with our corporate values.

Productivity

RWE is developing a company-wide data and AI strategy and has introduced its own ‘corporate GPT’. The aim is to automate and optimise processes in order to increase productivity and efficiency.

Energy Trading

In energy trading, RWE primarily uses AI to analyse weather data and forecasts. AI-based algorithms help traders to better predict energy prices and dynamically adjust trading strategies.

Maintenance & Inspection

RWE is increasingly using AI to optimise the maintenance and inspection of energy plants. The centrally bundled analysis of sensor data and operating information using machine learning models makes it possible to detect potential malfunctions at an early stage.
