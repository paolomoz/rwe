New wind power projects for global growth

RWE is among the largest players worldwide in the important sector of electricity generation from wind – and the company is poised for growth. With the comprehensive growth and investment strategy, RWE is expanding its high-performing generation facilities. To this end, RWE is investing billions of euros in expanding its generation portfolio, focusing on the Americas, the European core markets as well as Asia-Pacific to realise the well-filled project pipeline.

Many projects and new technologies are the result of research and development in the wind power sector. Development, construction, operation and marketing – RWE Renewables Europe & Australia provides a full range of wind power services under one roof. This makes the company a leader in developing competitive pilot projects.
