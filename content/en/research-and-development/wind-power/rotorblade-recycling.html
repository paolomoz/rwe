Rotor Blade Recycling

As a pioneer in offshore wind energy, RWE is increasingly focusing on sustainable innovations and is playing a key role in driving forward the development of recyclable rotor blades

Recycling in wind power: new paths to sustainability

Wind energy is considered one of the most important pillars in the transition to a sustainable energy supply. While the majority of a wind turbine is already easily recyclable today the rotor blades pose a particular challenge. They are made of complex composite materials that have been difficult to separate from each other. Currently, only a few recycling solutions are being developed to prevent decommissioned blades from ending up as waste in landfills. However, RWE is investing in a particular approach which focusses on "design for recycling". This approach takes sustainability into account right from the start of a wind turbine's life cycle. It also underlines RWE's ambition to improving circular economy by refurbishing turbine components whenever possible or by returning them to the raw material cycle, thereby conserving resources and protecting the environment.

Recycling process for rotor blades: Focus on technology

The key to the enhanced recyclability of Siemens Gamesa’s new rotor blades lies in an innovative resin. While conventional resins create a permanent bond, the new resin can be dissolved at the end of its service life – in a particularly gentle and efficient manner.

The process consists of four steps:

cp-prose

1. Decommissioning after end of life

At the end of their service life, the rotor blades are professionally removed from the wind turbines and prepared for recycling.

2. Immerse in mild acidic solution

The rotor blades are placed in a mild, heated acid solution. This dissolves the special resin, allowing the various materials (such as glass fibres, plastics, metals and wood) to be separated from each other.

3. Reclaim separated components

The now separated raw materials are cleaned, dried and then ready for reuse.

4. Re-use

The high-quality materials can now serve as a resource for a wide variety of products, such as components in the automotive industry, bicycle helmets or housings for electrical appliances.

Our sample projects: pioneering work for a green future

RWE is using the recyclable rotor blades in various large-scale projects, thereby actively contributing to the further development of sustainable wind energy:

cp-prose

Sofia Offshore Wind Farm (United Kingdom)

At our offshore wind farm Sofia, more than half of the total 100 turbines are being equipped with recyclable rotor blades – an internationally acclaimed showcase project. Thanks to close cooperation with Siemens Gamesa, recyclable rotor blades have been installed on a large scale in the UK for the first time, bringing the circular economy in wind energy within reach.

Kaskasi Offshore Wind Farm (Germany)

Kaskasi serves as a ‘research laboratory’ for sustainable wind power solutions. Among other innovations, the recyclable rotor bladeswere used here for the very first time, forming the basis for large-scale applications.

Thor Offshore Wind Farm (Denmark)

In the Danish Thor project, RWE is using recyclable rotor blades and steel towers made of CO2-reduced materials. The plan is to install a total of 40 turbines with 120 recyclable rotor blades, combining innovation and climate protection.
