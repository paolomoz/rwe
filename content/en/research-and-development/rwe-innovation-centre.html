Our Innovation Centre

A centre for the future of the energy supply system

How does CO2 scrubbing work? How can carbon dioxide be turned into a valuable raw material? RWE Power works on answering questions like these at its Innovation Centre at its Niederaussem power plant location. The company has already initiated major research and development projects dealing with the future of the energy supply system.

RWE Power set up the information centre in order to familiarise the public with these topics in an understandable, transparent and entertaining way. It also offers international experts the opportunity to engage in dialogue about world-leading technology.

The region researches and the world benefits

RWE truly brings this statement to life in Niederaussem. Through technical innovation, we wish to make our company viable for the future. That’s why we bundle our pioneering activities at the Niederaussem power plant location and have invested over €100 million in research and development in recent years. Our projects go straight from the site to the workbench, so to speak.
