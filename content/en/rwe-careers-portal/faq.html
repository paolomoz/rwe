Where and how can I apply?

You can find all advertised positions on our job portal. If a position is of interest to you, please apply only via our online application platform, which you can access from any job description by clicking on the ‘Apply now’ button.

Can I submit an unsolicited application to RWE?

Unfortunately, it is not possible to submit an unsolicited application at RWE, as our recruiting is a decentralised responsibility of the individual RWE operational companies. There is no central office within the Group that can check unsolicited applications and forward them to relevant departments - and also guarantee you a good application process. We hope you will nevertheless find what you are looking for in our job board. Thank you for your understanding.

What does the application process look like?

The process takes, depending on the role you would like to apply for, usually between 4-8 weeks and includes the following steps:

1. Online application

Once you’ve found a suitable position, apply exclusively online using the “Apply now” button in the respective job description. If you have all the documents ready, the application process will only take 3-5 minutes. Immediately after uploading your application, you will receive an automated confirmation of receipt.

2. Selection / Getting to know

The next steps in the application process depend on the individual position you’ve applied for. Generally you will receive initial feedback from us within two weeks after your application.

Your process may include the following steps:

  • Introductory meeting/ interview with the hiring manager and/ or a colleague from the HR department (virtually, by phone or in person)
  • (Mini-) assessment centre
  • Case study
  • Meeting the team

If you have any questions during the process, please get in touch with the contact named in the job description at any time.

3. Medical examination

Some roles require a medical examination. You’ll be informed in due course.

4. Contract

If it suits both parties, we will be happy to make you an offer and send you the contract. Please send it back as soon as possible. The entire process typically takes about 6-8 weeks.

What formats and file sizes are permitted for uploading documents?

You can upload files in .doc, .txt, .ppt, .pdf, .tif, .gif, .bmp, .jpg and .html formats.

We prefer PDFs. We regret that file formats not listed here, including .zip files, cannot be processed. The attachments must not exceed a total size of 5 MB.

How does a phone or virtual interview work?

A telephone or virtual interview is a great way to get to know each other and for us both to decide whether we are a good match.

We use Microsoft Teams for our virtual interviews and have had very good experiences with it.

How can I find out the status of my application?

At any time, you have the option of tracking your application's status in your candidate profile.

Alternatively, please always feel free to get in touch with the contact person.

Where will my place of work be?

The respective work location is always stated in the job description. For some positions, you can choose where you want to work. This is especially the case for positions where the teams work at different (often international) locations.

Is my job at RWE secured?

Sure!

We can say that quite clearly. RWE is one of the so-called "systemically important companies". We make sure that the energy supply in Germany and other countries is secure. Because we all need electricity - children and adults alike, small and large companies. Where there is electricity, there is light, heat and communication, there is production, medical care and mobility. Electricity is life.

How is RWE committed to the health of its employees?

We think it's important to ensure that we all stay healthy and fit. Because that's the only way we can also work together successfully towards a better future. That's why we rely on a strong company health management system that goes far beyond the norm. You have the opportunity to participate in numerous sports and seminar programmes. A great opportunity to have fun, do something for your health and get to know your colleagues better at the same time. Our company medical service provides comprehensive advice and care, both preventively and in the event of illness, and also offers an annual flu vaccination and various advisory services. We always refill your energy tanks - so that we can start together into a sustainable and healthier future.

For example on our RWE campus in Essen (Germany), the Health Center offers sports courses (such as yoga, functional training, personal training), prevention courses (such as back training) and equipment training. There are company sports groups and discounted memberships at Urban Sports and Fit X etc. in the Rhenish lignite area. We have many sport-enthusiastic colleagues who like to spend their break on a run or bike tour together. Showers are of course available on campus. For all other locations, the contact persons will be happy to answer your questions.

What is RWE's position on mobile / hybrid work?

Hybrid (mobile) working is an essential part of RWE’s work culture and is also handled flexibly wherever possible. Depending on the respective position, it can be arranged with the manager at any time.

Remuneration & benefits - when do I find out more?

We'll discuss salary and remuneration during the process of getting to know each other. And your benefits with us? You can find an overview here.

What apprenticeships does RWE offer?

We offer various different apprenticeships in the UK. For more details, please have a look at our UK career microsite.

What does the application process look like?

There will be variations for each role, but they might involve an application stage, (video) interview, and an assessment centre.

Where can I get more information?

We attend various career fairs and actively participate in apprenticeship activities, where you can make important contacts and learn more about our programmes. Our colleagues are happy to give you an exclusive behind-the-scenes look of an apprenticeship at RWE and answer your questions.

What will the assessment centre involve?

There will be variations for each role, but typically they’ll include an interview, a presentation exercise, and a group exercise with your fellow applicants. We’ll also provide you with the chance to meet managers to ask questions and assess our suitability for your future career.

What opportunities will I have after my apprenticeship at RWE?

RWE trains apprentices with a view to a long term role with the company. However, any permanent role after the apprenticeship will require the apprentice to apply for an available position towards the end of the course. There are various opportunities within RWE.

What does the application process look like?

The application process takes 4-6 weeks and usually includes the following steps.

1. Online application

Once you have found a suitable position, apply exclusively online using the “Apply now” button in the respective job description. If you have all the documents ready, the application process only takes 3-5 minutes. Immediately after uploading your application, you will receive an automated confirmation of receipt.

2. Selection

The next step in the application process is usually a virtual or in person interview with the hiring manager and/or someone from the team. You may be asked to do a short presentation or work on a case study but you’ll be informed in due course.

If you have any questions during the process, please get in touch with the contact named in the job description at any time.

3. Contract

If it suits both parties, we will be happy to make you an offer and send you the contract. Please send it back as soon as possible. The entire process typically takes 4-6 weeks.

Who can apply for an internship with us?

If you want to help us achieve our goal of "Our energy for a sustainable life," you've come to the right place. Diversity makes us stronger and you'll see many of our positions are open to students of all backgrounds.

We look forward to receiving your application if you have completed your 3rd semester (bachelor's degree), are in the gap year between bachelor's and master's degree, or are already a master's student.

All other requirements can be found in the respective job posting in our job board.

What internship duration does RWE offer?

An internship at RWE usually lasts 3-6 months. Longer internships are possible, e.g. during a gap year.

What do I earn during an internship at RWE?

The remuneration of an internship depends on the type of internship, i.e. whether you want to do a voluntary or compulsory internship and whether it is an internship as part of your bachelor's or master's degree. The contact named in the job description will be happy to provide you with individual information.

What are the requirements for a job as a working student?

You should ideally have completed your 3rd semester (Bachelor), be in the gap year between Bachelor and Master or already be in the Master program.

All other requirements can be found in the respective job posting in our job board.

What do I earn while working as a working student?

Of course we adhere to the applicable minimum wage, however in the end the remuneration depends on your previous experience and whether you are studying in your bachelor's or master's degree. The contact named in the job description will be happy to provide you with individual information.

Can I also write a bachelor's or master's thesis at RWE?

Occasionally, topics for bachelor's or master's theses are also advertised on our job board. Much more often, however, our interns or working students write their work with and for us. We therefore recommend that you first apply for an internship or a job as a working student and - if you like - discuss the topic directly in the interview.

What does the application process look like?

The application process takes up to 12 weeks and usually includes the following steps.

1. Online application

Once you have found a suitable graduate programme, apply exclusively online using the “Apply now” button in the respective job description. If you have all the documents ready, the application process only takes 3-5 minutes. Immediately after uploading your application, you will receive an automated confirmation of receipt.

2. Selection

Generally you will receive initial feedback from us within two weeks after your application.

The next steps in the application process are usually

  • Online test
  • (Time-shifted) video interview / job interview
  • Assessment centre

After completing each step successfully, you’ll be invited to the next one. The order of the steps may vary.

If you have any questions during the process, please get in touch with the contact named in the job description at any time.

3. Company medical examination

Some graduate programmes require a medical examination. You’ll be informed in due course.

4. Contract

If it suits both parties, we will be happy to make you an offer and send you the contract. Please send it back as soon as possible. The entire process typically takes up to 12 weeks.

What entry opportunities does RWE offer for graduates?

You can apply either for one of our graduate programmes or for a direct entry position. Find out more in advance on our pages for graduates.

You can find all open entry-level positions for graduates here in our job board. Use the filter to find the right job for you. If you did not find a matching career opportunity yet, please activate the job alert in order not to miss any new job ad relevant for you.

When can I apply?

The application process depends on the respective graduate programme. Please refer to the detail pages for more information.

You can apply for direct entry at any time. As long as the position is advertised, the application process is not yet completed.

Until when can I apply?

The application deadline for our graduate programmes also depends on the respective programme. However, we recommend that you submit your application as early as possible. Due to the high popularity of our programmes, we may have to close the application period earlier.

Some direct entry positions also have a deadline. You can find this in the respective job description on our job board.

Where will my place of work be?

The respective work location is always stated in the job description. For some positions, you can choose where you want to work. This is especially the case for positions where the teams work at different (often international) locations.

For our graduate programmes, a base location is agreed for contractual purposes. Depending on the programme, the location may vary. Take a look at the job description to find out more.

What kind of recruitment test is it?

The recruitment tests used may differ. You are welcome to find out more on the programme details pages or ask the respective contact person.

What should I expect at an assessment centre?

Assessment centres (AC) can also vary depending on the role you are applying for. Components may be:

  • Interview
  • Working through one or more case studies
  • Role play
  • Group exercise
  • Test

An AC is always a good opportunity to get to know RWE as a company and to get to know managers and colleagues. So take advantage of this opportunity and prepare questions for the colleagues who will be accompanying you.

Can I apply to multiple Graduate Programmes?

Please note that candidates can apply to one Graduate Programme per recruitment cycle. As each programme offers a different experience, opportunities and career path, we encourage you to carefully review the relevant job description and consider which programme best aligns with your skills, interests and career aspirations before submitting your application. If you submit applications to more than one Graduate Programme, you will be asked to select a single programme to progress with, and only that application will be taken forward for consideration.
