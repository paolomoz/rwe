Why work at RWE in Asia

RWE is one of the world’s leading energy companies, creating innovative solutions for a sustainable future – and you can help us bring that vision to life.

From our Asian headquarters in Singapore and offices in Japan, China, Indonesia and India, we connect global energy markets, optimise renewables and drive sustainable growth. Tech-led, agile and powered by passion, our culture thrives on trust, collaboration and impact. Here you’ll have the chance to grow with a team that’s expanding fast, backed by Europe’s largest energy trading floor.

Learn more about our Supply & Trading business.

What we offer

With 125 years of energy innovation behind us, we’re meeting this new age of sustainability and renewables with clear vision and international collaboration. No matter your background or talents, RWE is the place where your impact can be felt.

style

Training and development

We offer tailored learning, job rotations, career conversations and a strong onboarding experience. Our talent programmes support high-potential growth – helping our people take the next step, wherever they’re heading.

Committed to giving back

We’re proud to support the communities we’re part of. Across our locations, we get involved in local charity activities – encouraging our people to contribute time, energy and ideas to causes that matter. Giving back is part of how we create impact beyond the day-to-day.

Healthy mind and body

A healthy team is a happy team. That’s why we offer a range of health and well-being benefits – designed to support both body and mind. Depending on your location, this may include private healthcare, medical insurance, fitness activity reimbursements and access to mental well-being support.

Flexible work conditions

We recognise the importance of work-life balance and value the wellbeing of our team. Our guidelines serve to encourage uptake of flexible work arrangements, supporting us to structure our work time around outside commitments whilst still ensuring the delivery of our business goals.

Family friendly workplace

We know that life doesn’t stop when work starts. That’s why we offer a family-friendly environment with support that adapts to different needs and life stages. From maternity insurance coverage and parental leave coaching to flexible, agile working options – we make it easier to balance work and care.

Driving a sustainable future

As we better understand the energy needs of the world, we’re contributing to the success of the energy transition and the decarbonisation of the energy system. All in keeping with our purpose – Our energy for a sustainable life.
