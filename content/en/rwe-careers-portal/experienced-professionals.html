Application process

The process takes, depending on the role you would like to apply for, usually between 4 - 8 weeks and includes the following steps:

style
cp-prose, centered-heading, grey, plain
1. Online application

1. Online application

Once you’ve found a suitable position, apply exclusively online using the “Apply now” button in the respective job description. If you have all the documents ready, the application process will only take a 3-5 minutes.

2. Selection / Getting to know

2. Selection / Getting to know

The next steps in the application process depend on the individual position you’ve applied for. It may include

  • Introductory meeting/ interview with the hiring manager and/ or a colleague from the HR department (virtually, by phone or in person)
  • (Mini-) assessment centre
  • Case study
  • Meeting the team
3. Medical examination

3. Medical examination

Some roles require a medical examination. You’ll be informed in due course.

4. Contract

4. Contract

If it suits both parties, we will be happy to make you an offer and send you the contract. Please send it back as soon as possible. The entire process typically takes about 6-8 weeks. That's just the beginning. Now the real fun starts. Even before you join RWE, we'll start with your pre-boarding. We'll do everything we can to make it as easy as possible for you to join #TeamRWE.
