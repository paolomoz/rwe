Overview of programme

As the offshore energy sector evolves rapidly, we want motivated students ready to engage with real project challenges during the important construction phase – developing skills that raise their profiles and contributing effectively from the outset.

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact

Choose a placement at any stage of the offshore construction phase - from initial site preparation and marine logistics to structural installation and final documentation. Contribute to real offshore projects through coordination, reporting, planning, and document control, while gaining insight into the challenges of offshore construction.

Entry requirements

Curious, motivated individuals who are ready to get involved in real offshore projects. Build your confidence, skills, and industry insight with us.

  • Currently studying on a degree Engineering, Renewable Energy, Business, IT, or related fields, eligible for a Year in Industry
  • Effective communicators who thrive in cross-functional, diverse team environments
  • Willing to travel to project sites when needed
  • Organised and detail-oriented, with the ability to manage tasks and meet deadlines efficiently
  • Motivated to learn, take initiative, and make a meaningful contribution to the energy transition

Development & training

Our placement programmes are designed to equip you with practical experience and professional skills that can open doors to future career opportunities, including potential graduate roles.

From day one you will be embedded into a team and throughout your placement experience includes sustained guidance, specialised learning opportunities, and practical projects to help you thrive in a changing professional landscape.
