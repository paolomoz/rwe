Overview of programme

In a world focused on sustainability and climate action, we are searching for engaged students who are willing to confront future challenges and participate in the effort toward energy transition. Join #TeamRWE through our Engineering Placement Programme and discover opportunities for skill development, career advancement, and contributing to the evolution of offshore wind right from the beginning.

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact

During your Engineering Placement Programme, you’ll be based in one of our engineering teams such as:

  • Electrical Engineering
  • Technical Safety and Risk Management
  • General Engineering
  • Engineering Support

Each offers practical experience, typically including site visits and teamwork to support the safety, reliability, and performance of offshore wind farms, giving insight into managing complex offshore energy assets.

Key facts
  • Swindon, UK
  • Coventry, UK
  • UK Sites (e.g. London Array, Greater Gabbard)
Entry requirements

Curious and engaged thinkers ready to dive into offshore wind engineering. Gain practical experience and deepen your understanding with us.

  • Currently studying on a degree which includes a placement year, studying Electrical, Mechanical, Civil, Marine, or related Engineering degrees
  • Clear communication skills and confidence to collaborate
  • Organised, able to manage time and meet deadlines
  • Motivated to take initiative and contribute meaningfully

Development & training

Our placement programmes are designed to equip you with practical experience and professional skills that can open doors to future career opportunities, including potential graduate roles.

From day one you will be embedded into a team and throughout your placement experience includes sustained guidance, specialised learning opportunities, and practical projects to help you thrive in a changing professional landscape.
