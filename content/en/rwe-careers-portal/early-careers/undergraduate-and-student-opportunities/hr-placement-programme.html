Overview of programme

Our HR Placement Programme is the perfect match to gain a holistic view of the professional world of Human Resources at RWE. From here, you’ll get the chance to explore all HR-related areas and become a real HR expert in the energy sector.

Whichever path you choose, we’ll guide and support you as you take on interesting and exciting human-focused projects.

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact

During your HR Placement Programme, you’ll work within the Global Talent Attraction and Recruiting & Early Careers teams where you’ll have exposure to the full life cycle within a global organisation. You’ll also connect with other teams including People & Organisational Development, HR Operations and Reward for a complete picture of the different aspects of a fast-paced global HR function.

Entry requirements
  • Students who are in the penultimate year of a Business, Psychology, Business Education or similar degree, ideally with a focus on HR
  • Enthusiasm and drive to develop your skillset and seek out development opportunities
  • IT proficiency and excellent communication skills
  • Curious with a desire to work collaboratively
  • Motivation to take on new challenges, whether operating independently or in a team
  • Organisational talent and a strong sense of responsibility as well as solution-oriented, proactive action and creative drive

Development & training

Our placement programmes give you the chance to gain highly valuable work experience and skills – and potentially secure yourself a place on one of our graduate programmes. To help you get the most out of your placement, we provide lots of support and development opportunities, including in-house courses and on-the-job training with everything you need for success.

A current placement student will be allocated as a buddy to all new joiners to ensure seamless support as you begin your journey.
