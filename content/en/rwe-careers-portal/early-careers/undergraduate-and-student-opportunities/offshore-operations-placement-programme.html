Overview of programme

In a rapidly evolving energy landscape, we’re looking for engaged students ready to take on real operational challenges. Our Offshore Operations Placement Programme offers practical experience, industry insight, and the chance to work on a offshore wind from day one - because there’s no better way to truly understand wind farms than by being part of them.

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact

During your Offshore Operations Placement Programme you’ll be based in one of our Operations & Maintenance teams e.g. O&M Strategy, Support & Design or site-based Operations Engineering. Gain practical experience in maintenance planning, infrastructure, fault analysis, and day-to-day operations. Build real-world skills, contribute to impactful projects, and support the safe, efficient performance of offshore wind farms.

Key facts
  • Swindon, UK
  • UK Sites (e.g. North Hoyle, Rhyl Flats, Grimsby, Scroby Sands, Robin Rigg, Humber Gateway, Newhaven)
Entry requirements

Curious and engaged thinkers ready to dive into offshore operations. Gain practical experience and deepen your knowledge with us.

  • Currently enrolled in a degree programme that includes a placement year, studying Engineering, Operations, Renewables, or a related field, and eligible for a Year in Industry
  • Communication skills and confidence collaborating in diverse teams
  • Willingness to travel as needed
  • Systematic in approach, demonstrating effective time management and the skill to satisfy deadlines
  • Motivated to take initiative and make a meaningful contribution

Development & training

Our placement programmes are designed to equip you with practical experience and professional skills that can open doors to future career opportunities, including potential graduate roles.

From day one you will be embedded into a team and throughout your placement experience includes sustained guidance, specialised learning opportunities, and practical projects to help you thrive in a changing professional landscape.
