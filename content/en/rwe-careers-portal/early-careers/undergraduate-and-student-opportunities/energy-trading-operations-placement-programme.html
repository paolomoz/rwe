Overview of programme

From here, you can choose a whole world of experience.

Looking to gain experience at a leading European energy trading house with real growth potential? Want to work alongside industry experts, building experience and skills that will help you flourish in your early career? Then our year-long Energy Trading Operations Placement Programme could be just what you are looking for.

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact
  • Automated Trading & Surveillance Risk: Managing market abuse risk and algo trading governance to protect our licence to trade
  • Back Office: Smooth execution and settlement of our energy portfolio, including post-trade activities like regulatory reporting and collateral management
  • Commercial Asset Optimisation: Optimising and trading for thermal and renewable assets, including algorithmic trading projects
  • New Business Partners: Driving global growth in new markets with new product implementations and managing the Global Approved Product database
  • Risk Governance: Providing transparency on risk management through reporting and limit framework oversight
  • Trading Risk: Analysing Profit & Loss, trading strategies and market risks, and advising senior stakeholders
Entry requirements
  • Students who are in the penultimate year of a highly analytical, numerical or business-focused subject
  • Enthusiasm and drive to develop your skillset and seek out development opportunities
  • IT proficiency and excellent communication skills
  • Curious with a desire to work collaboratively and challenging yourself to find new solutions
  • Motivated to take on new challenges
  • Commercial aptitude and entrepreneurial skills as well as an analytical mindset and the ability to learn quickly
Swindon, UK
  • Phase 1: 21.09.2026 - 18.10.2026
  • Phase 2: 21.09.2026 - 23.11.2026

Development & training

Our placement programmes give you the chance to gain highly valuable work experience and skills – and potentially secure yourself a place on one of our graduate programmes. To help you get the most out of your placement, we provide lots of support and development opportunities, including in-house courses and on-the-job training with everything you need for success.

A current placement student will be allocated as a buddy to all new joiners to ensure seamless support as you begin your journey.
