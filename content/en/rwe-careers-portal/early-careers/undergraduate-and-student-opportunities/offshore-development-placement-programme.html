Overview of programme

Step into the unique world of offshore renewable energy with our Development Placement Programme. Created for motivated students, this opportunity provides practical experience in essential development fields like Environment, Social Value and more!

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact

Choose a placement immersed in the essential early stages of offshore wind development – from environmental assessments and planning to stakeholder engagement and consent management. Engage in actual projects by managing activities, evaluating data, writing reports, and maintaining records, while also learning about the regulatory, environmental, and social challenges impacting offshore wind development.

Key facts
  • Swindon, UK
  • UK Sites (e.g. Ramsgate, London Array, Grimsby)
Entry requirements

Curious, motivated individuals who are ready to get involved in real offshore projects.

  • Enrolled in Supply Chain, Business, Engineering, Environmental Science, Social Studies or related fields
  • Effective communicators who thrive in cross-functional teams
  • Organised and detail-oriented with effective task management skills
  • Self-directed and engaged, focusing on how this role contributes to your career development

Development & training

Our placement programmes are designed to equip you with practical experience and professional skills that can open doors to future career opportunities, including potential graduate roles.

From day one you will be embedded into a team and throughout your placement experience includes sustained guidance, specialised learning opportunities, and practical projects to help you thrive in a changing professional landscape.
