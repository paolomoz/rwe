Overview of programme

Looking to gain experience at a leading European energy business with real growth potential? Want to work alongside industry experts, building experience and skills that will help you flourish in your career? This scheme provides a balance of training and competency development; including technical, practical and business skills.

Our year-long programme could be just what you are looking for!

style
cp-prose, centered-heading
The area(s) where you can make an impact

Current opportunities vary of a yearly basis and may include the following placements:

  • Mechanical Engineering (Rotating Equipment)
  • Mechanical Engineering (HRSG and Balance of Plant)
  • Metallurgy 
  • Chemical and Environmental Engineering (Pembroke)
  • Process/Chemical Engineering (Biomass)
  • Electrical Engineering
  • Control & Instrumentation Engineering 
  • Information and Analytics 
  • Outage Project Planning and Delivery Support
Entry requirements

We would like our candidates to have an outstanding analytical, problem-solving mindset, a desire to learn and develop whilst being a team player.

Academically to be considered for our scheme you are required to be:

  • On course or be predicted to achieve a 2:1 or higher
  • In your penultimate year of an Engineering, Economics or Computer Science / Engineering degree (placement dependant)

Development & training

Our placement programmes offer unrivalled work experience and skills – and potentially secure a place on our graduate programmes. To help you get the most out of your placement, we offer support and development opportunities, combining academic learning, technical training and practical skills development, supported by experienced engineers, technicians and training specialists.

A current student will be allocated as a buddy to new joiners to ensure seamless support as you begin your journey.
