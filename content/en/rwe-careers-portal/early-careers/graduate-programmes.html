Application process

The application process takes up to 12 weeks and usually includes the following steps.

style
cp-prose, centered-heading, grey, plain
1. Online application

1. Online application

Once you have found a suitable graduate programme, apply exclusively online using the “Apply now” button in the respective job description.

If you have all the documents ready, the application process only takes 3-5 minutes. Immediately after uploading your application, you will receive an automated confirmation of receipt.

2. Selection

2. Selection

Generally you will receive initial feedback from us within two weeks after your application.

The next steps in the application process are usually

  • Online test
  • (Time-shifted) video interview
  • Assessment centre

After completing each step successfully, you’ll be invited to the next one. The order of the steps may vary.

If you have any questions during the process, please get in touch with the contact named in the job description at any time.

3. Company medical examination

3. Company medical examination

Some graduate programmes require a medical examination. You’ll be informed in due course.

4. Contract

4. Contract

If it suits both parties, we will be happy to make you an offer and send you the contract. Please send it back as soon as possible. The entire process typically takes up to 12 weeks.

When can I apply?

The application process depends on the respective graduate programme. Please refer to the detail pages for more information.

You can apply for direct entry at any time. As long as the position is advertised, the application process is not yet completed.

Until when can I apply?

The application deadline for our graduate programmes also depends on the respective programme. However, we recommend that you submit your application as early as possible. Due to the high popularity of our programmes, we may have to close the application period earlier.

Some direct entry positions also have a deadline. You can find this in the respective job description on our job board.

Where will my place of work be?

The respective work location is always stated in the job description. For some positions, you can choose where you want to work. This is especially the case for positions where the teams work at different (often international) locations.

For our graduate programmes, a base location is agreed for contractual purposes. Depending on the programme, the location may vary. Take a look at the job description to find out more.

What kind of recruitment test is it?

The recruitment tests used may differ. You are welcome to find out more on the programme details pages or ask the respective contact person.

Can I apply to multiple Graduate Programmes?

Please note that candidates can apply to one Graduate Programme per recruitment cycle. As each programme offers a different experience, opportunities and career path, we encourage you to carefully review the relevant job description and consider which programme best aligns with your skills, interests and career aspirations before submitting your application. If you submit applications to more than one Graduate Programme, you will be asked to select a single programme to progress with, and only that application will be taken forward for consideration.
