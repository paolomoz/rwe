Apprenticeships and training schemes

At RWE, learning and doing are one and the same. We’re helping drive the energy transition and innovate for the future of sustainable and renewable power – so there is always something new for us to discover.

Learn while you build a career with impact

Are you looking for a role that allows you to join an exciting industry, while developing skills that will serve you for life? You may be looking for an RWE apprenticeship or one of our other career starters schemes!

No matter which area you work in, RWE will provide you with the support you need so that you can achieve your best — at our operational sites, in the classroom and at the office.
