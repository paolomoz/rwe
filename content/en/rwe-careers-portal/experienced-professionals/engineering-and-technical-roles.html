Engineers

As an engineer at RWE, you play a vital role throughout the entire project lifecycle – from development and construction to operation – working with cutting-edge technologies in wind, solar, hydrogen, gas, and battery storage. As part of our skilled and dedicated team, you contribute to the successful delivery of every project by assessing risks, maintaining the highest quality standards, overcoming technical challenges, and continuously optimising the performance of our assets.

During the development phase, you assess site feasibility, considering environmental conditions, topography, and infrastructure. Your work supports key tasks such as soil analysis, foundation design, and grid connection planning.

In the construction phase, you ensure quality standards are met, adapting to challenges like unexpected ground conditions or changes in grid capacity.

Collaboration is key to success. Working closely with our teams across project development, construction, and procurement, you help meet technical, commercial, and legal requirements while turning innovative ideas into efficient and sustainable energy solutions.

You’ll also focus on advancing new technologies and optimising the operation of our power generation facilities. By modernising and enhancing our assets, you help ensure a reliable energy supply.

Our teams bring together engineers from diverse disciplines, including mechanical, electrical, civil, mechatronic, and water engineering. Together, we’re shaping the future of sustainable energy.

Technicians

With your technical expertise, you play a crucial role at RWE, ensuring our wind, solar, hydrogen, gas, and battery storage facilities are always up to date and operating smoothly and efficiently – from regular maintenance to essential modifications and component replacements.

You carry out both preventive and reactive maintenance activities, always ready to tackle technical challenges with innovative solutions. Working independently in small teams, you use advanced monitoring and diagnostic systems, documenting the performance of our facilities to plan the necessary next steps.

Safety is our top priority: our teams adhere to the highest Health, Safety, Security & Environment standards and actively contribute to continuously improving the performance and safety of our facilities.

At RWE, we offer you the chance to work in a dynamic, forward-thinking industry and to collaborate with us on innovative, sustainable energy solutions.
