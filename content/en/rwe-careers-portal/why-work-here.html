Why work at RWE

We’re the collective energy of about 20,000 people, all working together around the world to inspire a brave new legacy and help transition towards sustainable energy for the future. We’re proud of our diverse #TeamRWE – because whatever your origin, identity, religion or talent, taking proper care of the climate and environment is a vital issue that affects all of us. As we innovate and develop new energy projects, we also believe in investing in the growth and support of our people.

Your energy has impact. At RWE, use it for good.

style

With more than 125 years of energy innovation behind us, we’re meeting this new age of sustainability and renewables with clear vision and international collaboration. No matter your background or talents, RWE is the place where your impact can be felt.

Driving a sustainable future

As we better understand the energy needs of the world, we’re contributing to the success of the energy transition and the decarbonisation of the energy system. All in keeping with our purpose – Our energy for a sustainable life.

Local initiatives, global results

Driving the sustainable energy transition for millions means there’s no insignificant effort towards our goals. By taking on local projects around the world, each effort builds to greater impact and new discoveries.

We back you up

To innovate for the future of energy, we ensure our people are supported in a culture that encourages respect, growth and unique perspectives. At RWE, team spirit means sharing your ideas, being heard and building on experiences that support progress and continue our legacy.

Our differences inspire us

Diversity of experiences, backgrounds and ideas keeps us strong and resilient. We’re committed to creating an environment where everyone is valued – and can add value.

Be a part of a global player

Drive the energy transition with one of the world's leading renewable energy companies. With us, you’ll benefit from a wide range of development opportunities, a global network, and valuable experience in a forward-thinking industry – the ideal foundation for your personal and professional growth.

Benefits for every phase of life

Our wide-ranging benefits package is designed to support you in every aspect of your professional and personal life. Tailored to your career level, personal needs, and interests, we offer benefits that not only make your worklife easier but also promote your overall well-being.
