Our colleagues at RWE Renewables Europe & Australia are united by one thing above all: their passion and commitment to a sustainable future. At all our locations, whether in Europe or Australia, colleagues are working together to drive the energy transformation forward.

At RWE, we offer a hybrid work culture that enables our colleagues to achieve an optimal work-life balance. In exciting and inspiring stories, they talk about their challenges, successes and the special culture at RWE. Immerse yourself in the world of our onshore and solar experts and find out how we are working towards a greener future with innovative strategies and great commitment.

Join us on this journey and get to know the people who make a difference at RWE every day. Because our work is more than just a job for us - it's a mission.
