Products and Services

RWE – a strong global partner

With a portfolio of offshore and onshore wind, solar, hydropower, biomass, gas and lignite, RWE covers the entire field of secure and clean energy supply. Wherever expertise in the realisation of energy projects is required, RWE Technology International, which specialises in project management and engineering services, supports its clients from the industrial and commercial sectors. These services range from the construction and dismantling of entire plants to the modernisation of buildings and the use of renewable energies.

Further subsidiaries, including RWE Supply & Trading, RWE Renewables Europe & Australia and RWE Power, also offer various products and services under the roof of RWE AG. From green electricity contracts to emissions trading: In our "All products & services at a glance" section below you will find an overview of the range of products and services of RWE.
