Environmental protection

Our environmental commitment

At RWE, protecting the environment goes hand in hand with transforming the energy system. As outlined in our Code of Conduct, we believe in handling natural resources responsibly and promoting the use of environmentally friendly technologies.

Strict environmental legislation and permit requirements build the framework for our operational activities in the regions where we are active. Some of our activities even surpass the obligations set out in laws and permits.

To control our activities related to the environment, we have set up an environmental management system in affiliated companies that have relevant operating facilities. We follow the international environmental standard ISO 14001, while factoring in country-speciﬁc and statutory requirements. Environmental auditors assess our environmental performance on a regular basis, identify associated risks and opportunities and use these results for continuous improvement measures.

Our focus

Our environmental commitment is focused on three priorities: climate, biodiversity, and the circular economy. We drive systematic change by reducing emissions, enhancing ecosystems, and using resources in an efficient manner.

We have set clear targets, integrated them into our business processes, and we are monitoring progress. Where there are standards, we align with them, and where they are being developed, we contribute actively to their development.
