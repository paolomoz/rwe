Social affairs and society

Taking responsibility as part of society

To RWE, taking social responsibility means actively addressing issues, such as the protection of human rights, diversity and sustainable supply chain standards. Health protection, supporting the development of our employees and the safety of residents are also top priorities for us. We therefore continuously involve ourselves in the community by initiating social projects, being in constant dialogue with stakeholders and supporting our employees’ development through numerous campaigns and seminars.
