The Policy Statement on RWE’S Human Rights Strategy forms the basis for the implementation of human rights standards at RWE.

The Policy Statement was released by the Executive Board of RWE AG on December 20, 2022.

It supplements and specifies the requirements for compliance with human rights from our code of conduct.

The Human Rights Policy Statement is available in different languages.
