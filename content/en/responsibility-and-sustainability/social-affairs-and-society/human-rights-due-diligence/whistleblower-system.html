Our aim is to ensure that human rights and the environment are respected in our own group companies, at our partners and at our suppliers. In order for us to be able to do this successfully, we depend on knowledge of potential risks and violations of human rights and environmental protection being reported to us.

If you are aware of any potential or actual violations of human rights and environmental protection, or have even been affected by human rights violations yourself, you have the opportunity to report them via various channels.
