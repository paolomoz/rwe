Transparency and accountability are at the heart of our operations.

We have created a central complaints channel accessible to all. This offers individuals or community groups the opportunity to register their concerns and complaints through the contact form below or anonymously if desired. We aim to create a confidential space for community members to communicate their thoughts, ensuring that their voices are heard and valued.

We want to hear what you have to say.

Your feedback are at the centre of our shared journey toward a more environmentally responsible and sustainable future. We want to support a culture of openness and inclusion actively. You are welcome to contact us in your local language.

By listening to you, we have the opportunity to learn, adapt, and continually improve our practices.
