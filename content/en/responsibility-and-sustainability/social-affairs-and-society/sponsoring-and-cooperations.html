Sponsoring & Cooperations

Sponsored by RWE

RWE is firmly rooted in the regions surrounding our operating and administrative sites. It is therefore a matter of course for us to provide financial support to clubs and charitable organisations in the immediate vicinity. The promotion of projects in the areas of popular sport, culture, education and the common good is one of the focal points of our sponsorship commitment. There is also a special focus on the preservation of customs in the Rhineland region. We are a strong local partner.

PV system with combined battery storage at SIGNAL IDUNA PARK in Dortmund

RWE to become a “Premium & Sustainability Partner” of Borussia Dortmund. As part of the joint project, the photovoltaic system on the roof of Borussia Dortmund’s SIGNAL IDUNA PARK stadium will be modernised and expanded.

Ruhr Piano Festival

The Ruhr Piano Festival is the cultural flagship project of the Initiativkreis Ruhr. RWE's sponsorship also supports the associated foundation, which has been carrying out important educational work for over ten years. In this way, the creative skills of children and their interest in classical music are developed.

indeland triathlon

RWE supports events in the region in order to increase the range of leisure activities on offer. We are the main sponsor of the indeland triathlon. Every year, the triathlon attracts almost 2,000 athletes and numerous visitors to the indeland countryside.

Manheim Karting Club

RWE sponsors clubs in the region that focus on promoting young talent. The Manheim Karting Club has developed a concept for promoting young talent in the Bambini and Junior classes that is based on sustainability.

Shirt sponsorship

With the "RWE fulfils jersey dreams" project, we support sports clubs in purchasing jerseys for their youth teams. The teams come from the communities near our operating sites and play ball and team sports such as football, volleyball, basketball and handball. The promotion of youth teams is particularly important to us.

Cultivation of customs

The Rhineland region is characterised by strong cohesion and good neighbourly relations with RWE. The village communities are characterised by joie de vivre and traditions: cherished customs are cultivated. RWE supports this by sponsoring local associations and their festivals.
