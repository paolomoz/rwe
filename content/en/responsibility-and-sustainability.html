We are a company in the midst of a fundamental transformation:

phasing out coal, scaling renewables, and integrating sustainability across our business. We are growing green – and sustainability is at the core of this transition.

As part of society and a key player in the global energy market, we recognize the responsibility that comes with our role. We follow an integrated approach that responds to environmental challenges, cares for people, and creates long-term value. Our sustainability strategy is guided by ambitious targets, a clear action framework, and full transparency.
