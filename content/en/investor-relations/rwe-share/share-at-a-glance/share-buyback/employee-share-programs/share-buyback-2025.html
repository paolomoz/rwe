Share Buyback October to December 2025 Employee Shares
Description

Theme

In the period from 6 October 2025 to 2 December 2025 RWE Aktiengesellschaft intends to acquire a maximum of 700,476 shares to serve the sole purpose of meeting obligations arising from an employee share programme.
