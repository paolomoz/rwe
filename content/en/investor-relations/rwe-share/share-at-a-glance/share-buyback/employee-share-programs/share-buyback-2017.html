Title
Share Buyback November 2017
Description
Information and downloads about the share buyback 2017.
Theme
solid-header

In the period from 7 November 2017 to 10 November 2017 RWE Aktiengesellschaft bought back in a first tranche in total 206,500 common shares with a value of approx. 4,738,893.45 EUR. In a second tranche in the period from 13 November 2017 to 15 November 2017 RWE AG bought back in total 134,420 common shares with a value of approx. 2,895,439.64 EUR. The buybacks were made in order to fulfill obligations relating to an employee stock option plan and are completed now.
