Dividend

Dividend proposal for past fiscal year: €1.20 per share.

In view of the Group’s strong earnings, the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of RWE AG will propose paying a dividend of €1.20 per share for fiscal 2025 to the Annual General Meeting that will be heldon 30 April 2026. In the second half of this decade, we intend to raise the dividend by 10% annually. The dividend envisaged for fiscal 2026 is thus €1.32.
