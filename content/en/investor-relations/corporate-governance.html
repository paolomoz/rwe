Corporate Governance at RWE

Responsible business management and monitoring are the cornerstones of long-term success. Our guiding principle is the most recent version of the German Corporate Governance Code, which was introduced in 2002. We fully comply with the recommendations of the Code, helping to strengthen the trust that investors, customers, employees and the public have in us.

The complete text of the code as well as further information concerning the code are available at

www.dcgk.de
