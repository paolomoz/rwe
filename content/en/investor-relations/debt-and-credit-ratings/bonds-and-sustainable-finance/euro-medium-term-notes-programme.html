Euro Medium Term Notes Programme

style
article-stage

RWE’s “Euro Medium Term Notes" program (EMTN) is capped at EUR 15 billion. It is updated once a year and supplemented in the course of the year if it becomes necessary. Our EMTN program includes both RWE AG and RWE Finance Europe B.V. as issuers. The EMTN prospectus is listed at the Luxembourg stock exchange and has been approved by the Commission de Surveillance du Secteur Financier.
