Credit Ratings

RWE holds investment-grade ratings with Moody’s since 2000 and with Fitch since 2003. RWE is committed to maintain strong investment grade ratings. Our deliberate financial policy and prudent liquidity management have led to stable ratings over the past years.

Our ratings will be regularly updated by both agencies over the course of the year.

Please note that ratings may be revised, suspended or withdrawn at any time by rating agencies.
