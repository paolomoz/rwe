Bonds & Sustainable Finance

Debt capital markets serve as a key funding source in supporting RWE’s growth strategy. Our sustainability ambitions are underpinned by the regular issuance of green Use of Proceeds bonds, of which all proceeds are allocated to EU Taxonomy eligible technologies.

Our business activities are financed through senior and subordinated issuances in Euro and U.S. Dollar. Both markets offer us diversification and access to a broad investor base. We prioritize issuing in markets where we have established operations, however opportunistically we are ready to explore other markets if favorable funding conditions arise.

Engaging with investors and promoting the understanding of RWE’s credit profile is of the highest importance to us. We value the support investors have provided to our growth intentions. Therefore, our dedicated fixed income investor relations team is ready to answer questions and provide further information at your request.
