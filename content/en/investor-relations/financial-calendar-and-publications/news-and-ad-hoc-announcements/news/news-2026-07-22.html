Essen, 22 July 2026

  • Turbine installation well underway with testing and commissioning taking place step by step
  • Full commissioning of Nordseecluster A, with 660 megawatts of capacity, scheduled for early 2027; Nordseecluster B to add further 900 megawatts of capacity from 2029
  • Nordseecluster expected to generate around 6.5 terawatt hours of electricity per year, helping to decarbonise the industry
