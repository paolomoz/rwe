Great Britain’s T-4 capacity auction for delivery in 2029/30 has concluded: RWE secured 6,373 MW of capacity agreements

style
article-stage

Essen, 10 March 2026

  • The auction cleared at £27.10/kW/year
  • 34 RWE assets successfully secured 1-Year Agreements
  • 4 RWE assets successfully secured 3-Year Refurbishment Agreements
  • 1 RWE asset successfully secured a 15-Year Agreement

The T-4 Capacity Market auction has concluded, for delivery in 2029/30. The EMR Delivery Body (NESO) has announced that the capacity auction has cleared at £27.10/kW/year, and 40,108.608 MW of capacity has been secured for Great Britain. 

A provisional list of the GB assets that have successfully secured an Agreement can be viewed here. This information is still subject to Secretary of State approval.

RWE has secured agreements for all of the Capacity Market Units (CMUs) that were entered into the auction. This means RWE has 6,373 MW (derated) of capacity agreements beginning in the 2029/30 delivery year. 

RWE has been successful in securing T-4 agreements for a range of technologies including: 17 1-year agreements and four 3-year refurbishing agreements for the gas fleet and another 16 1-year agreements for the hydro, wind and battery storage CMUs which were entered. Additionally, one 15-year agreement was secured for a battery storage project. Both battery storage CMUs will be co-located with solar. 

The GB Capacity Market remains a cornerstone of the country’s energy market design and has provided stability for power generation companies over the past decade. As older renewable sites become eligible to enter the Capacity Market auctions, this will offer an important additional revenue stream—supporting long-term viability.

RWE is the UK’s largest operator of Combined Cycle Gas Turbine (CCGT) power plants, with a modern and efficient fleet of around 7GW installed capacity, ensuring security for the country’s energy supply. The company is developing carbon capture and storage options at four sites, reinforcing its commitment to decarbonisation.
