Remuneration of the Executive Board and the Supervisory Board of RWE AG

style
article-stage

Executive Board

The current remuneration system for the members of the Executive Board of RWE AG is in place since fiscal 2025. The Annual General Meeting endorsed the remuneration system on April 30, 2025, with a majority of 93.92 %. The resolution documents can be found in the Invitation to the Annual General Meeting, and the remuneration system can be found here.

style
cp-prose

Supervisory Board

The currently applicable remuneration regulations for the Supervisory Board are set forth in Sec. 12 of the Articles of Incorporation of RWE AG based on the remuneration system for the Supervisory Board. They were adopted by the Annual General Meeting on April 30, 2025, with a majority of 99.62 %. The resolution documents can be found in the Invitation to the Annual General Meeting, and the remuneration system for the Supervisory Board can be found here.
