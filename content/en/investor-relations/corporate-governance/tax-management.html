A sound and transparent management of our tax liabilities and tax compliance issues is an essential part of our governance systems. We are aware that we have the responsibility to contribute to common value added in the countries we are operating in – not only by law but also due to societal expectations. Our tax policy is one pillar to meet these expectations and to present a clear and obligatory scope of actions to our employees and our external stakeholders with regard to tax issues.
